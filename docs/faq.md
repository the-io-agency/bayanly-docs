# FAQ

## What happens to my translations if I uninstall?

Translations are saved in your Shopify store, so they stay. The app's own data is deleted 48 hours after uninstalling.

## Does the app slow down my store?

No. Translations are served by Shopify. The app embed only loads on pages in a translated language that have custom content, and loads after the page.

## Does the app translate automatically?

No. You write or import your own translations, so you stay in control of the wording.

## Why isn't a translation showing on my storefront?

* Check that the language is **published**.
* Check that the translation is **saved**.
* Make sure you're viewing the language's URL (for example `/fr`).
* For custom content and images, the **Translations** app embed must be on in your live theme.
