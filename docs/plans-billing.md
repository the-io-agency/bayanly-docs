# Plans & billing

Every plan includes every feature. Your plan follows your Shopify plan:

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly (2 months free) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $5 | $50 |
| Grow | $10 | $100 |
| Advanced | $15 | $150 |
| Plus | $20 | $200 |

* Every plan starts with a **15-day free trial**. Development stores are free.
* Charges appear on your Shopify invoice.
* Change your plan, switch between monthly and yearly, or cancel at any time on the **Plans** page.
